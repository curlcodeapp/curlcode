# Camille Rose — verified source discrepancies (2026-11-20)

**Status:** Eight flagged records cross-checked against their individual Camille Rose product pages. This verifies the manufacturer's published text, not physical package label or experimental efficacy. The downloadable local report `camille_rose_discrepancies_verified.zip` has a QA overlay CSV and source URLs. The original 63-product draft remains intact.

## Corrections / holds
- **Rose Cleansing Milk:** Official directions require rinse-off after cleansing scalp and hair; change erroneous `leave_in` to `cleansing_milk`. Store generic cleansing capability; no automatic FR25.
- **Nangai & Tsubaki Strength Restore Protein Treatment:** The brand explicitly calls it protein treatment, but the published INCI comprises oils/extracts and fragrance and contains no readily identifiable protein ingredient; directions describe scalp/length oil application. Reclassify functionally as hair/scalp oil and withhold FR06. A similarly composed Nangai & Tsubaki Hair Oil listing exists; preserve separate source URLs pending duplicate/SKU review.
- **Spiked Honey Mousse and Mini:** Official INCI lists differ materially (full-size PVP near fifth position versus mini with rice water/honey near top). Keep separate formulation variants; packaging-level verification remains open.
- **The Gro Grease:** Official page makes psoriasis, dandruff, eczema and hair-growth claims; displayed INCI contains behentrimonium methosulfate, a quaternary hair conditioning agent, but no obvious elemental sulfur active. Do not treat 'methosulfate' as evidence of sulfur-treatment efficacy. Do not promote FR19 or hair-growth claims without appropriate evidence.
- **Brown Butter Melt variants:** Official listed INCI sequences now match after normalizing old spelling differences; likely same formulation. Keep SKU/URL provenance but do not present as independent formulation alternatives.
- **Black Castor Oil Essence Pure Strengthening Oil:** Official INCI is water-first, with black castor oil later; promotional text describes a near-pure oil and mentions coconut oil absent from displayed INCI. Flag **internal manufacturer source inconsistency** and classify according to actual listed formula, not product name.

## Limits
Manufacturer pages can themselves disagree; no physical bottle label or production-batch verification yet. Do not silently change source text. These corrections overlay the provisional 63-product catalog; unreviewed products remain provisional.
